# Axio Biosolutions > Axio Biosolutions is a medical technology company focused on advanced chitosan-based solutions for bleeding control, trauma care, surgical care, surgical bleeding management, and advanced wound care. The company develops and manufactures products such as Axiostat and MaxioCel to support rapid hemostasis, wound healing, infection risk reduction, and improved outcomes across emergency, military, hospital, surgical, and wound care environments.
